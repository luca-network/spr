# Maintainers

## Now

| Role | Who | Contact |
|---|---|---|
| Editor | A representative of Luca | hello@luca.network |

While SPR is a draft, the editor is the only maintainer. As `CONTRIBUTING.md` explains, this repository is published from Luca's maintained source with each release, so no one commits to it directly, Luca's own engineers included. Accepted pull requests are applied by the editor and appear in the next release, with your authorship kept.

This is a transitional arrangement for the draft stage. It ends in the steps below.

## How someone outside Luca gets commit rights

1. **Reviewer.** Anyone with three accepted contributions (pull requests or RFCs) in the past twelve months can ask the editor to be listed as a reviewer. Reviewers are asked to review pull requests and RFCs in their area, and their approval is recorded in the decision.
2. **Maintainer.** When the working group described in `GOVERNANCE.md` forms, it nominates maintainers from its members and the reviewers, and at least one of them is from an organization other than Luca. From then on, the specification, the test vectors and the reference library are maintained in this repository directly: maintainers merge pull requests here, and Luca's releases are built from this repository instead of the other way round.
3. **Standards body.** When a recognized standards body takes over SPR, as `GOVERNANCE.md` sets out, its own process decides the maintainers, and this file points to it.

Removal follows the same path in reverse: a reviewer or maintainer who has not taken part for twelve months, or who asks to step down, is moved to the list of former maintainers below.
