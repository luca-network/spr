# Patent commitment for SPR

Luca, the publisher of SPR at github.com/luca-network/spr and luca.network, makes this commitment so that anyone can implement SPR, the Sealed Payment Record, with or without Luca's reference code, without needing a patent license from Luca.

## The commitment

Luca will not assert any Covered Claim against anyone for making, using, selling, offering for sale, importing or distributing a Covered Implementation, or for any part of a Covered Implementation to the extent it implements the specification.

## What it covers

- **Covered Claims** are the claims of any patent or patent application, in any country, owned or controlled by Luca now or later, that are necessarily infringed by implementing the required or optional parts of the specification. A claim is necessarily infringed only when there is no technically reasonable way to implement that part of the specification without infringing it.
- **A Covered Implementation** is software or hardware that implements SPR as published by Luca at github.com/luca-network/spr: draft 1, and any later version published there with this commitment. The commitment covers the parts of the implementation that implement the specification, and nothing else in the same product.
- **The specification** is `SPEC.md` in that repository, at a version published with this commitment.

## When it ends

The commitment ends for you, and for any company you control or that controls you, if you or it starts or joins a lawsuit or other proceeding claiming that a Covered Implementation infringes a patent. It ends from the day the claim is made. It continues for everyone else.

## Other terms

- **It is irrevocable.** It cannot be withdrawn, except as described under "When it ends".
- **Later versions.** Luca may publish different terms, or no commitment, for any later version of the specification. A change applies only to the versions published with it, and never reduces the commitment for a version already published.
- **It binds Luca's successors.** It binds anyone to whom Luca transfers a Covered Claim, and anyone who takes over Luca's business.
- **It needs nothing from you.** You do not have to sign anything, register, or tell Luca that you rely on it.
- **It is about patents only.** It grants no trademark rights, including in the names Luca and SPR, and no rights in any other patent.
- **The code has its own license.** The reference library, the checker and the test vectors are licensed separately under the Apache License 2.0 (`LICENSE`), which includes its own patent license for that code. This commitment adds to that license and does not change it.
- **It makes no promise about the code.** Luca gives no warranty that an implementation does not infringe someone else's patents.
- **Liability.** To the fullest extent the law allows, Luca is not liable for any damages of any kind arising from this commitment, from the specification, or from implementing it. Where the law does not allow that exclusion, Luca's total liability to any person or organization, worldwide and for all claims together, will not exceed US$9.99.
